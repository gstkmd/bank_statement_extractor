ShieldDocs Help & Guide

                
            

            
            

                
🌐 Running on the Network

                
ShieldDocs can be accessed from any computer on your local office network without needing to install it on each machine.

                
Simply open a web browser on another computer and enter this URL:

                
http://<SERVER_NAME>:5055

                
Note: If you have issues, you can also use this computer's local IP address (e.g., http://192.168.1.10:5055). Ensure your firewall allows inbound connections on port 5055.


                
🛡️ How to use the Redaction Feature

                
                

                    Privacy Guarantee: The local AI works entirely on your system. No data or document contents are ever sent to any third party. You can verify this by disconnecting your system from the internet and trying it out yourself!
                


                
    
                    
  1. Upload Document: Go to the "Redact" tab and drag-and-drop your file (PDF, Image, Word, TXT, etc.) into the designated area.
    2. 
                    
  2. Analyze: Click the "Analyze document" button. The local AI will automatically scan your document for sensitive information like names, IDs, dates, and amounts.
    3. 
                    
  3. Review & Edit: You will see a table of suggested redactions. The AI replaces real data with realistic dummy data. You can review and modify the dummy text if needed.
    4. 
                    
  4. Manual Selection: If the AI missed anything, scroll to the "Raw Document Text" section. Select the sensitive text with your mouse and click "Add Selected Text" to redact it.
    5. 
                    
  5. Approve: Click "Approve & Redact" to generate the final anonymized document. You can download the redacted file securely.
